Privacy notice

Launch draft: Sobers Pizza should review and approve this wording before the public launch.

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Information we use

To prepare and fulfil an order, Sobers Pizza may use the customer’s name, phone number, order details, delivery address, notes, payment status and order history. Optional accounts may also store an email address, profile details and favourites.

Why we use it

  • To confirm, prepare, deliver and support orders.
  • To prevent fraud and resolve payment or service disputes.
  • To send promotions only when the customer permits them.
  • To improve menu availability and customer experience.

Location and notifications

Location access and browser notifications are optional and require device permission. A customer may decline them and continue browsing and ordering. Permissions can be changed in the device or browser settings.

Sharing and storage

Information is shared only with service providers and authorized staff needed to operate the ordering service, such as hosting, database, authentication, payment and delivery providers. Sobers Pizza does not sell customer information.

Security and retention

Reasonable access controls and secure connections are used. Order records may be retained for operations, accounting, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Information should be removed or anonymized when it is no longer required.

Your choices

Customers may browse as guests, update optional profile details, sign out, disable notifications, or request help with account information by contacting Sobers Pizza through the official contact shown in the app.
