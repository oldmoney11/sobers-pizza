Ordering

Customers may browse as guests. An order is submitted only after the checkout details are confirmed. Availability, preparation time and delivery estimates may change during busy periods.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in Ghana cedis. The final total shown at checkout includes selected sizes, extras and any stated delivery fee. Use only the official payment details displayed in the app or confirmed by Sobers Pizza. Keep your order reference and payment receipt.

Delivery and pickup

Customers are responsible for providing a reachable phone number and accurate delivery information. Delivery timing depends on distance, traffic, weather and order volume. Pickup orders should be collected using the official order reference.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

Contact Sobers Pizza promptly if an order must be changed or cancelled. Once preparation has started, cancellation or refund eligibility may be limited. Approved refunds are returned using an appropriate agreed method and may require payment verification.

Food and allergens

Tell Sobers Pizza about allergies before ordering. Food may be prepared in a shared kitchen where common allergens are present, so complete separation cannot be guaranteed.

Acceptable use

Do not submit fraudulent orders, misuse payment details, attempt unauthorized access or interfere with the service. Sobers Pizza may reject abusive or suspicious activity.

Support

For questions about an order, use the phone or WhatsApp contact shown in the app and provide the official order number.
