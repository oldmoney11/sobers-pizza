On the road

  • Keep this app open while the order is on the way so the customer can see your point.
  • Use Navigate for the route. Do not type while riding.
  • Do not post a customer’s phone number or address.
  • If you feel unsafe, do not continue the drop. Call 112, then call the kitchen.

Help

The whole run

  1. Sign in and wait until Sobers approves your account.
  2. Tap Go online. A new ready order rings for about 10 seconds. Silence ring turns the sound off and leaves the Inbox alert on.
  3. Tap Accept, collect the food, then tap Pick up and start.
  4. Allow location. The customer tracking page shows your latest point.
  5. Tap Navigate if you want Google Maps directions to the address.
  6. Tap Mark delivered only when the customer has the order.

If an order is missing

You must be approved and online. The kitchen must mark the order ready. Another rider may already have accepted it.

If the map is blank

The customer map opens only after you tap Pick up and start. Allow location for this site and keep the phone awake.
